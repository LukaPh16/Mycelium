## What the project is

**Mycelium** is an Android app that shows which apps on your phone secretly contact which companies on the
internet, and turns it into a living **pixel-art terrarium**.

- You pick the apps you want to watch (e.g. TikTok, Instagram, Glovo). Only those apps go through Mycelium.
- Mycelium sees every **contact** an app makes (technically: DNS lookups, the "phone book" step before connecting),
  recognizes which ones belong to **trackers and ad networks**, and shows it all in plain words.
- Each watched app becomes a **cute creature** living in a glass terrarium on a windowsill.
- Trackers appear as **"Snoops"**: little pests (a tick-mite, a moth, a leech) that latch onto the app's creature.
  The more an app feeds trackers, the **sicker** its creature looks: red blotches, then creeping veins, then a
  sickly color. **Red dotted threads** flow from each Snoop up and out through the jar's lid, showing your data being
  carried away "to feed your feed".
- Tap a Snoop and **"Shoo it away"**: Mycelium blocks that tracker company for that app. Poof, sparkles, hearts,
  and the creature **heals**.

### Privacy (the main message)
- "Your data is kept and processed on your device." Capture, classification, stats and alerts all run on the phone.
- No accounts, no analytics, no servers of ours. The VPN feature Android provides is used **locally only**; nothing
  is sent to a VPN server.
- Honest limits (we show them in the app on a "What Mycelium can't see" page): it sees names of websites, never
  contents; some apps hide their lookups (private DNS / hardcoded addresses); it never stops all tracking.
- Optional AI helper uses the **user's own OpenRouter key**, only when they ask, and only sends a short summary of
  the facts behind that question (viewable under Details). Without a key, the chatbot works fully offline.
- Optional local weather (off by default) is the only other network use: rounded ~11 km location to Open-Meteo.

### Features to show
- **Terrarium (home):** full-screen pixel art (inspired by the cozy feel of games like Owlboy, but 100% original
  art generated by our own scripts). Glass jar, cork lid, moss hills, pond, blossom tree, floating mini islands.
  - Creatures: one per app, **25 creature types** (fly agaric, moon jelly, cactus, slime, crystal…), each app gets
    its own colors and a little hat.
  - **8 growth stages** (Seedling → Sprout → Young → Grown → Blooming → Elder → Ancient → Legend). Growth comes from
    the app's activity; later stages take days or weeks. Each stage adds details (flowers, mushroom friends,
    glowing spores, a golden halo).
  - **Evolution moment**: when a creature reaches a new stage, a full-screen animation plays (light rays, flicker
    between old and new form, burst, fanfare).
  - **Moods**: happy, content, grumpy (a new Snoop just arrived), feeling sick (trackers took over), napping (app
    quiet for 3 hours). Shown as little speech bubbles with matching behavior and sound.
  - Hold or drag a creature to move it; it lands on the nearest surface. Pinch / double-tap to zoom.
  - **Weather and seasons**: the window behind the jar shows rain, thunderstorms with lightning, snow, fog, clouds;
    the sun and moon cross the sky with the real time of day (moon in its real phase). Seasons recolor the jar:
    spring blossom, summer green, autumn amber with falling leaves, winter frost with a snow-globe flurry.
  - **Sound**: every creature type, Snoop and animation has its own original synthesized sound, plus cozy weather
    ambience (rain on the window, crickets at night). Volume/mute settings included.
  - Bottom sheet: "N Snoops visited your garden today", pets / Snoops / shooed counts, creature cards.
- **Ask the Gardener:** a chatbot that answers questions like "What just happened?" or "Which apps talk to Meta?"
  from on-device data, with every fact linked to real recorded events. Works offline. With the AI helper on, it
  can also explain things in depth and give tips (labelled as general knowledge).
- **Activity:** friendly timeline cards grouped by app ("A Snoop came by: Braze", "2 min ago").
- **Insights:** busiest app, most Snoop visits, night-time visits, data used, a pixel bar chart (today / this week).
- **My Apps:** pick apps with big switches; reset an app's record; change its creature.
- **Safe Mode (offline):** with no internet, Mycelium answers apps locally, queues what it caught, and sorts it
  when you're back online.

### Real numbers we saw while testing on a phone (safe to quote)
- In one afternoon, **TikTok made 165 contacts**; **Instagram had 10 Snoop visits** (Meta); **Glovo** talked to
  the trackers **Braze** and **Adjust**; TikTok met **AppsFlyer**.
- Our tracker list recognizes 186 tracker/ad domains from companies like Google, Meta, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Braze.

### Tech (one short sentence is enough in the video)
Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, a local VPN that only reads DNS for chosen apps, on-device SQLite database,
on-device classification, stats and anomaly detection; everything reachable through one JSON API so other UIs
(like our Lovable UI) can plug in.

## Practical notes for the shot list
- Record on a real phone in portrait, Do Not Disturb on, battery/clock tidy.
- Before recording: watch 2–3 apps and use them for a while so the jar has Snoops; the Terrarium needs real data.
- For the evolution moment, the debug build can trigger it:
  `adb shell am start -n app.mycelium/app.mycelium.app.MainActivity --es evolve <package>`.
- Weather can be set manually in Settings > Weather (Rain, Storm, Snow…) and season in the same section.
- Sounds: record with media volume on (Settings > Sound) if the video keeps app audio under the voiceover.
